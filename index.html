FairCast Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

FairCast does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Your files (photos, videos, music, screen) travel directly from your phone to your TV over your own Wi-Fi network. They are never uploaded to any server.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. FairCast only learns whether the unlock has been bought; it never sees your payment details.
  • Permissions: network access (to find and talk to your TV), screen capture (only while you choose to mirror, with Android's own consent prompt), record audio (only while mirroring, to send the sound of apps playing on your phone to your TV; nothing is saved or uploaded), and notifications (to show the Stop button while casting).
  • Remote control: button presses and text you type are sent straight to your TV over your local network. They are not stored or sent anywhere else.
  • FairCast Receiver (TV app and Roku channel) collects nothing either; it only plays what your phone sends over your local network.
  • Children: FairCast is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
